# MADINA AI TRADER PRO

## Institutional AI Trading Engine for MetaTrader 5

---

# Mission

MADINA AI TRADER PRO is a professional institutional-grade Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5 focused primarily on XAUUSD.

This project is intended to become a premium commercial trading engine rather than a traditional retail Expert Advisor.

The long-term objective is to maximize profitability while maintaining controlled drawdown through adaptive AI-driven decision making, Smart Money Concepts and professional software architecture.

---

# Project Philosophy

The project must evolve like a professional trading platform.

Architecture always has higher priority than adding new features.

Every module must remain modular, scalable, maintainable and configurable.

Long-term stability is more important than short-term optimization.

---

# Main Objectives

- Maximum profitability
- Low drawdown
- Capital protection
- Institutional AI
- Professional architecture
- High performance
- Scalability
- Maintainability
- Full configurability

---

# Trading Principles

The EA must never behave like a simple indicator robot.

Every decision should be based on complete market analysis.

Multiple confirmations are always preferred over single-signal trading.

The objective is quality rather than quantity.

# Profit Protection

Traditional trailing stop should be replaced with adaptive profit protection.

The objective is not to minimize stop distance.

The objective is to maximize retained profit.

Strong trends should be allowed to continue.

Weak trends should trigger increasingly aggressive profit protection.

---

# Risk Management

Risk must be adaptive.

Risk depends on:

- Drawdown
- Confidence
- Win Rate
- Profit Factor
- Volatility
- Liquidity
- Trend Quality
- Market Regime

Martingale, Grid and Averaging are permanently prohibited.

---

# Performance

The EA should remain lightweight.

Requirements:

- Cache expensive calculations.
- Minimize CopyBuffer calls.
- Prefer new-bar calculations.
- Reuse calculated data.
- Avoid duplicated logic.

---

# User Configuration

Every configurable parameter should become an Input whenever technically possible.

All user-facing Inputs and descriptions should be written in Uzbek.

Internal implementation remains English.

---

# Development Rules

Never rewrite stable modules.

Never duplicate logic.

Never duplicate indicators.

Never create temporary fixes.

Never break compatibility without measurable benefit.

Always improve architecture before adding functionality.

Always verify compilation before considering work complete.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The final product should resemble a professional institutional trading engine rather than a traditional MetaTrader Expert Advisor.

Every future improvement should increase:

- Quality
- Scalability
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Performance

without sacrificing trading stability.
